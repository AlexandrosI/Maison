The Architecture

Built the way the island was built.

Whitewashed walls thick enough to hold the day's cool inside them. Arched doorways that soften every silhouette. Marble and stone underfoot, polished by centuries of Parian feet.

The vocabulary is old — Cycladic, monastic, patient. Every detail is chosen so nothing competes with the light coming off the water, or the record turning in the corner.

Inside Bebop

A white room that keeps the noise out.

We designed the room to disappear. The walls are white, the floor is stone, the furniture is simple. The music is warm and the light is soft. The only thing that matters is what you came for: the food, the wine, the company.
