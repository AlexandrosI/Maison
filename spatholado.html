Spatholado

Spatholado — St John's wort oil — is recognised for its many properties. It was a famous cure of the Ancient Spartans, who healed their wounds with it after battle, and it remains an “essential medicine” from early times until our days.

The flowers bloom from the beginning of May until June. Officially Hypericum, it is one of the most common flowers of Greek flora and grows on uncultivated land. The plant is distinguished not only by its bright yellow colour but by its antidepressant, antiseptic, antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, astringent and healing properties. It also helps with sciatica, rheumatic pain, joint pain, bruises and first-degree burns, and is ideal for therapeutic and relaxing massage.

Its preparation is simple. The flower must be picked in the morning and prepared freshly harvested. The spatholado matures in the sun for about 40 days, macerating in high-quality olive oil, turning a deep red before being stored in a bright spot.

Here we insist on this: the secret comes from the olive oil, and we use the oil we produce ourselves. Spatholado found elsewhere is often poor quality, made with various seeds and oils that give it an unnatural colour. It should never be stored in plastic — only glass — and keeps for about a year.

NOTE: The oil must not be used during the day in the sun, nor by pregnant women.
