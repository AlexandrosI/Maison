Getting here

Every way to arrive

✈︎

From Athens airport

The direct road from 'Eleftherios Venizelos' International Airport to the port of Ermioni takes 3 hours by car or by bus.

⛵︎

By ferry from Piraeus

Two hours by ferry from Piraeus to Ermioni. If there is little time between your flight arrival and the ferry's departure, we can arrange a taxi to take you straight to the port of Piraeus without any waiting time — this does not affect the fare.

🚗︎

By car

Head towards Corinth, then Epidaurus; after that the route is clearly signposted. The road is scenic and in good condition. We recommend hiring a car at the airport or in Athens — there are few hire companies here and they are often very expensive.

🚌︎

Transfers

If you would rather not hire a car, we can arrange your transfer from Ermioni harbour or from the bus stop in Kranidi. Please don't hesitate to ask for our rates.
