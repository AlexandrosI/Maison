the room

One room, entirely yours

Spacious (20 m²) and very bright, with its own private entrance by an external staircase, a genuine king-size bed, a separate private bathroom, and direct access to two very large verandas facing the sea.

Tea and coffee are always waiting for you, the Wi-Fi is free, and the only sound at night is the wind through the olive trees.

George is a third-generation beekeeper — the hives sit around our farm at Petrothalassa. Our olive oil comes exclusively from our own grove, and the marmalades are made by hand, in a copper pot, from fruit picked the same day.
