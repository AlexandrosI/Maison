Our Philosophy

"For us, sushi is simple: a few fresh ingredients prepared with great care.

Bebop × Joomla is a cozy spot in Parikia inspired by two passions: the precision of Japanese food and the free spirit of jazz music.The Aegean Sea brings them together.

We take our time with everything — from the fish and the rice to the music and your drink. We prefer to do a few things perfectly, rather than many things just okay."

The Sounds

From spiritual jazz to deep electronic beats.

The sounds of our curated playlist create an immersive atmosphere, enhancing every moment of your dining experience. There is no algorithm at Bebop × Joomla.
